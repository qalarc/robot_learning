# Assets and Lineage

**This repo is a distillation of a dozen prior projects — this page evaluates each one: what it is, what we actually take from it here, its feasibility verdict (use now / borrow idea / reference only), and where it lives in this repo.** Facts from [docs/03_our_assets_map.md](../docs/03_our_assets_map.md), [docs/03b_unity_armsmith.md](../docs/03b_unity_armsmith.md) and [reviews/](../reviews/).

## Conventions inherited

Ports (ours: bus 8890, sim 8891, preview/dashboard 8892; Laya 8798, GUT console 8797), formats (three.js r186+ ESM vendored, GLB, JSONL decision rows, games.json-style catalogs), style (code-generated worlds, no art packs; small boring working processes; archive, never delete).
